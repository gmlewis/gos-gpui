What is new, and it is one function used twice:

  * `gpui-binding/src/app.rs` — `menu_plan(items)` reads a menu's entry list into the items
    it describes, numbering an `"events:"` entry's slot by counting **events entries**.
    `process_requests` now *builds* the menu from it, and `gpui::menu_slots` *reports* it, so
    the wiring and the reader cannot drift apart — the reader is not a second opinion about
    the numbering, it is the same code.
  * `gpui-binding/src/lib.rs` — `gpui::menu_slots(items)` answers `"<slot>:<label>"` for every
    `events` entry, and `set_menus`'s doc, which said "maps entries onto them by position",
    now says what it means: by counting events entries, with a separator taking no slot.
  * `"quits"` with no label now defaults to `"Quit"` rather than building an item with an
    empty name.

**A behaviour change worth naming.** A program that put a separator in front of an action
used to see that action answer `"menu|2"`; it now answers `"menu|1"`. That is the fix, and it
is the kind of change that wants saying out loud rather than discovering. No example is
affected: `examples/17_set_menus.gos` passes `["separator", "quits:Quit"]` and has no `events`
entry at all.

can reach them; recorded in `## Unreachable` beside the other upstream absences.

What the check is not: it does not click a menu. Whether the action a slot is wired to is
the one that *fires* needs a menu bar and a human, which is W8's standing doubt as well.
