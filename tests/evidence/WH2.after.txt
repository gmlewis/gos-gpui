WH2 — maximized / fullscreen at open, `toggle_fullscreen`, `zoom_window`.

AFTER THE WORK. In this tree:

    TIER_TIMEOUT=45 ./scripts/tiers.sh tests/gui/wh2_window_state.gos

Output, identical on all five tiers:

    OK WH2 a window opened with start=maximized reports itself maximized
    OK WH2 zoom_window put the window back, and the platform reports it

The probe opens its window with `start=maximized`, waits for the platform to say so
through `gpui::window_option("maximized")` — which is `Window::is_maximized`, the
window's bounds being the display's work area — and then calls `gpui::zoom_window()`
and waits for the platform to say *that*. Both directions are the platform's own
answer, not the binding's record, which is why this row's check is behavioural where
WH1's, WH3's and WH8's cannot be.

Two measurements worth keeping, both taken while writing the probe:

  * **The zoom takes about 23 frames.** `start=maximized` is applied on the
    window's first frame — `options::take_start` is consulted there — but
    `Window::is_maximized` only becomes true when the animation lands, measured at
    frame 23 (frame 22 was still false, frame 23 true) on a 60 Hz display. That is
    why the deadline is a *time* and not a frame count.
  * **An occluded window stops being painted.** In the deliberate break below, the
    window is never zoomed, and frames stopped around frame 40 with the process
    still alive — so the probe's deadline was never reached and the run had to be
    killed. This is the row's residual doubt, and it is a property of every probe
    here that waits in a window it does not keep on top.
