**Three failures, and one assertion that passes at HEAD and is worth naming.** The child's own two
presses *are* heard at HEAD — `inner|down` reads 1 and then 2 — because the old reading of `kinds`
is `kinds.contains("down")`, and `"down!"` contains `down`. So the stop was never a missing
*listener*: the listener was always there and only the stop was absent, which is why this probe's
first FAIL comes from the parent and not from the child. What fails is exactly the other half — the
parent hears the press the stop should have withheld (1, then 2).

**The third FAIL is the control, and it is the one that makes the other two readable.** The press
posted past the child's own rectangle is one the parent *should* hear, and at HEAD the parent's
tally is 3 rather than 1 — it heard all three. Without that reading, "the parent heard nothing"
would be satisfied by a parent that had never registered anything, which is the failure mode that
makes a stop look like it works. At HEAD the two are the same story from opposite ends: the parent
heard everything, including the two it should not have.
