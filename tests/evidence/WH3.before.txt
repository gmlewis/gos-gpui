The field exists upstream and is unused here. `WindowOptions::app_id:
Option<String>` is `gpui-0.2.2/src/platform.rs:1123`, and on macOS
`set_app_id` is deliberately a no-op (`platform/mac/window.rs:1255`,
`fn set_app_id(&mut self, _app_id: &str) {}`) because the concept belongs to the
Linux desktop entry. That is why the *check* is the reader rather than an
observation: there is nothing on this platform to observe. What the row asks for
is that a program can name its application at all, and that the name reaches the
`WindowOptions` the window manager on the other platform will read.
