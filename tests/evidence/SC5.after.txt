What makes it pass:

  * `gpui-binding/src/list.rs` (new) — one `UniformListScrollHandle` per
    `(window, list id)`, in a thread-local map, beside `scroll.rs`'s and for the
    same reason.
  * `gpui-binding/src/view.rs` — the `Kind::Virtual` arm calls
    `.track_scroll(handle)` on the `uniform_list`, **and records the range gpui
    hands the row-builder closure**. That closure argument is the rows that are
    on screen, and it is the only reliable statement of them: gpui's own
    `UniformListScrollHandle::logical_scroll_top_index` is
    `#[cfg(any(test, feature = "support"))]`
    (`gpui-0.2.2/src/elements/uniform_list.rs:218`) and does not exist in a
    normal build.
  * `gpui-binding/src/lib.rs` — `gpui::list_range(id) -> (first, last)` and
    `gpui::list_scroll_to(id, index, strategy) -> bool`, the latter answering
    `false` both for a list nothing has drawn and for a strategy that is not
    `top`, `center` or `bottom`.
