**Three failures, and the nine tallies that are not among them.** The probe also asserts, per
gesture, how many presses, releases and synthesised clicks the box heard — nine readings in
all — and every one of them passes at HEAD. That is the shape of this row: `down`, `up` and
`click` were always delivered, and the only thing missing was the number the platform had
already computed. A probe that asserted "a click arrived" would have been green at HEAD and
evidence of nothing.

**`-1` is a sentinel, not a count.** `<id>.count` does not exist at HEAD and the probe reads it
through `gpui::state_int(.., -1)`, so a missing slot is distinguishable from a slot holding
zero. Zero is a real reading — it is what a press that continues no run would be if the
platform counted that way — and the sentinel is what keeps the two apart.
