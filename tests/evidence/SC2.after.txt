A container whose content fits reports 0, and an id nothing has drawn reports 0;
the probe therefore uses a container it knows overflows — `h=100` around a
`h=400` child — so a zero answer is a failure rather than an ambiguity. The sign
is gpui's: positive, the amount of content past the container, which is the
denominator a scrollbar thumb divides by.
