MR4 — after (stop): a press that stops where it landed, and what a stop withholds.

The row is "interactive-element event completeness: click variants, mouse up/move/exit,
`stop_propagation`, group hover", and this file is about its `stop_propagation` clause. The other
three clauses are `tests/evidence/MR4.after.txt` (the click count), `tests/gui/km7_pointer_beyond.gos`
(the element-level `exit`) and `tests/evidence/MR4.group.after.txt` (group hover).

gpui has always had the call — `cx.stop_propagation()`, read by `Window::dispatch_mouse_event`
(`gpui-0.2.2/src/window.rs:3677-3723`) — and nothing in the binding could make one. A program
raised events and heard them; which of its own listeners a handled press was withheld from was
not something it could say.

--- why the stop is spelled on the kind, and which way it points

`gpui::on_mouse` reads `kinds` as words, and the word for a stop is the kind it belongs to plus a
`!`: `"down!"` is `down` **and** a stop, `"down! up"` reports both and stops only the press. It is
spelled on the kind rather than as a separate call because the two are the same listener
registering for the same event — a program that wanted a stop but not the report, or a report but
not the stop, can say either. A `!` that did *not* also register the plain kind would be the worst
of both: a modifier that silently disabled the listener it was written on.

**What a stop withholds the event from is the ancestors, and that is the reverse of the obvious
reading.** gpui's dispatch runs a capture pass over the listeners in registration order and then,
`if cx.propagate_event`, a bubble pass over the same list **reversed**, breaking out of each the
moment the flag goes false (`window.rs:3695-3714`). Elements register in paint order, and a div
registers its mouse listeners at `elements/div.rs:1863` **before** it builds its children at
`:1870` — so a parent is earlier in the list than its child, the reverse is child-first, and a stop
in a child is heard before the parent ever is. `stop_propagation` therefore means "the panel around
me does not also react", which is what a control that handles its own presses wants. It is about
the one event and not the next: `Window::dispatch_event` resets `cx.propagate_event` for every
event it dispatches (`window.rs:3584`).

--- the probe, and the third press that makes it mean something

The tree is a child `rect` inside a parent `rect`, both listening for presses; the child asks for
`"down!"` and the parent for `"down"`. Two presses land on the child and the child hears both; the
parent hears neither. **Those two readings alone would also be what a parent that never registered
anything says**, so the probe posts a third press past the child's own rectangle — inside the
parent and outside the child — and the parent hears that one. The same listener, in the same tree,
in the same run: the silence is the stop.

The probe is `// container:` — it drives the pointer through XTEST — so `scripts/probes.sh` skips it
on this Mac and the five container tiers are its witness.

--- the window-level half

`gpui::on_mouse_window` takes the same `!`, and it stops among the window's own listeners rather
than among elements. A window listener runs in gpui's bubble pass over `Window::mouse_listeners`
(`window.rs:3695-3714`), so `"down!"` there withholds the press from the window listeners
registered **before** it and does not touch an element's listener at all. The two halves are the
same word doing the same thing to two different lists, and the reader is told which list they are
stopping in, because reaching for the window-level one is usually a sign that the gate on
`gpui::element_bounds` is the thing wanted instead.

--- what upstream asks for it, and what this row did not add

`26_window_shadow.gos` is the port that carries `stop_propagation`, and its `Deviations:` bullet is
classified **`invisible`** in the census: upstream's client-decoration arm is dead on macOS and the
port renders the Server arm, so the stop there never fires in either. The clause was built because
the register's row names it, not because a pair needs it — and the row is the specification.

No new trait or wrapper was needed. The listener that reports is the listener that stops; the stop
is one call inside the six kind arms of `Kind::Mouse` in `gpui-binding/src/view.rs`, plus the same
call in `register_window_mouse`, and the two places read `kinds` through one shared reading of the
`!` suffix.
