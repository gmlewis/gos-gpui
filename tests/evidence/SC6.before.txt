    FAIL SC6 a list nothing has scrolled reports rows -1..-1, not from row 0

`(-1, -1)` is the sentinel `list_range` answers for a list it has never laid out,
which is why the probe's first assertion is that the range starts at row 0 rather
than merely that it is non-empty: the sentinel would otherwise pass.

the way past rather than read back later:

place it is reliably visible.
