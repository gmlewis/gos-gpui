WHAT WAS ALREADY THERE, checked against gpui rather than against the row's
wording. The register lists four things; two are complete because gpui has no more
to give them:

  * **wrapping** — `gpui::WhiteSpace` has exactly two variants, `Normal` and
    `Nowrap` (`style.rs:321-327`), and the binding's `ws=` reaches both. There is
    no `pre`, `pre-wrap` or `pre-line` in gpui 0.2.2 to reach.
  * **clamping and truncation** — `clamp=` is `line_clamp(usize)`; `truncate`,
    `ellipsis` and `textoverflow=` cover `TextOverflow`, which has exactly **one**
    variant, `Truncate(SharedString)` (`style.rs:331-335`). Complete.

WHAT WAS MISSING, and is now in:

  * **per-line spacing** — `lh=` was a multiple of the font size and nothing
    else, so a fixed 22-pixel row could not be asked for. `DefiniteLength` is
    exactly those two shapes and only one was exposed.

Every one of those is **focus order**, not text layout: `tab_stop.rs` is the sum
tree of focus handles behind `Window::tab_index` and `Window::tab_stop(bool)`,
which the binding already reaches as the `tabindex=` and `tabstop` style tokens.
`mod tab_stop;` is private, so even that machinery is not callable from outside
the crate, and `Styled` has no tab-related method at all — the single match in
`styled.rs` is inside the word "mutable". **A text tab stop cannot be exposed,
because gpui does not have one to expose.**
