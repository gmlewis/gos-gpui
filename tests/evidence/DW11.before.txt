DW11 — before: the row is red on both halves, and they are red for different reasons.

The row asks three things of a `w=auto` box: that it is its content plus its padding, that
its run of text can be measured and hit-tested, and that a tooltip raised over it is sized
to its own content. At HEAD (329133c) **none** of the three can be read out of a window:

  * a box that listens to nothing has no box at all — `element_bounds` was recorded from a
    pointer listener's prepaint, so a box with nothing to listen with answered `0`, and
    "how wide did this come out?" had no answer;
  * the `bounds` token does not exist, and neither do `gpui::text_x_of_index` /
    `gpui::text_index_at`;
  * and a tooltip's subtree, which gpui lays out in a *later* frame than the one that
    described it, was not drawn at all.

Read the `check` line as the reason this row stayed open so long: `gos check` does not
resolve `gpui::` binding names, so six faults — one unknown style token and five names
that do not exist — pass a type check and only surface at `run` (`gpui: noted:`) or at
`build` (`undefined symbols before LLVM tools`). The probe that says so is
`tests/tmp_dw11_red.gos`, kept only until this transcript was taken.

That is clause one, isolated: `38.816` of text plus `2 × 8` of padding is `55`, and the box
that came out `55` is the only one of the two that carries a listener. The box that
measures nothing is the one the row is about.

--- and clause three, isolated: a tooltip at HEAD

The tooltip probe cannot even be written at HEAD — `gpui::tooltip` is not a name the
binding has — so its red is the whole probe failing to resolve, which is the same fault
the check above reports. What *can* be said about the tooltip half at HEAD is where it
went wrong when it was first built against the *new* binding, because that is a fault
this row's fix had to find: a tooltip is the one element gpui draws that the frame did
not hand it. Its builder runs on gpui's own half-second clock, in a task
(`elements/div.rs:2751`), and the view it answers is laid out inside a *later* frame's
`Window::draw` (`window.rs:2088`) — after `Root::render`, the one place `window::with`
points the thread at a window, has already returned, and after `view::reset` has already
emptied the arena that described it. So at HEAD it drew as an empty box, and the note the
binding printed once per frame was:

       gpui: noted: child handle 4 names no element in this window (window 1 holds 1)

`tests/evidence/DW11.after.txt` is the run where that note is gone.
