This row's capability **is** the platform opening windows, and nothing comes back to the binding — no return
value, no event, no state — so the instrument had to be the person watching. The probe makes that an instrument
rather than a shrug: two buttons, whose presses arrive as events and land in the transcript above. That is the
whole of the evidence, and it is honest evidence: a human confirmed the two observable effects, in order.

Residual doubt: what Finder or the text editor *drew* was not inspected, only that they opened the right thing.
`gpui::reveal` and `gpui::open_with_system` return nothing, so a platform that silently ignored the call would
produce the same transcript as one that honoured it — except that the person watching would have said so. Tiers:
`check`, `run`.
