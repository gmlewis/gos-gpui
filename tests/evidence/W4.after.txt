AFTER THE WORK. Command, in this tree:

    TIER_TIMEOUT=40 ./scripts/tiers tests/gui/w4_requests.gos

Output, identical on all five tiers (`check` prints `check: ok (2 items typed)`;
each of the other four exits 0 with no stderr):

    OK W4 two windows ticked the same id at 30ms and 5000ms, and each heard only their own

What makes it pass: `app.rs`'s timer registry is keyed by `(window, event id)`,
so the child's `tick("beat", 5000)` is no longer dismissed as a duplicate of the
parent's `tick("beat", 30)`; and the events each loop raises are pushed into its
own window's queue (W3), so the child — whose own timer is five seconds out and
does not fire before the probe ends — hears nothing at all.

The probe is bounded by frames, not by wall clock: the parent quits at frame 40
having required at least one beat of its own, and the child fails on the first
beat it hears that it did not raise.
